One disrupted passenger, end to end
Flight EZY LGW→BCN cancelled · best man at a wedding tomorrow
1
Nothing to explain. Flight LGW→BCN is cancelled and the customer opens the app. Ada already knows the flight, the PNR and the disruption reason.
2
“Will you pay for my hotel?” Ada answers with this customer’s entitlement — not a link to an FAQ: covered costs, receipt limits, claim timeline.
3
Ada completes it. One call through the MCP layer into Levarti / the Disruption Hub: hotel confirmed, rebooking options offered, EU261 claim opened.
4
Judgement stays human. Welfare edge cases — unaccompanied minor, medical need — escalate to a Dynamics agent with the full transcript and context attached.
Why the fulfilment engine is already there
TA Connections at easyJet, live since February 2023: £14M projected first-year saving · 74% of refunds auto-processed · 80% of passengers self-serving against a 55% target · 44,000+ self-service hotel bookings. The engine and its APIs exist — the gap is an authoritative answer on entitlements before the customer reaches fulfilment. That is the gap Ada fills.
